## Draft system basics that affect variance

- **Glycol chillers** and **insulated trunk lines** keep long-draw beer cold. A warm line means foam, and foam means loss.
- **Gas blenders / regulators** set the right pressure. Wrong pressure means foam or flat beer.
- **FOBs** (foam-on-beer detectors) stop foam when a keg empties, so product stays in the line instead of going down the drain.
- Cooler target: BeerSAVER's exception range flags **below 30°F or above 42°F**. Most draft programs aim for 36–38°F.
